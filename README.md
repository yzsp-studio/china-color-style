## 🇨🇳 中文

把一个故事前提（年代、人物、场景、情绪）或一张现有图片，变成可直接执行的 AI 出图美术方案：定哪条朝代/画风流派并给出视觉依据；给出带真实 CMYK/RGB 数值与占比的传统色配色；补齐服饰形制、色阶规矩以及必须避开的坑；最后组装成按主体/风格/媒介/光线分段的英文提示词，复制即可用。数据资产自包含在技能自己的 data/ 目录里，384 个传统色按二十四节气组织并附出处章节与书页；模式 C 可以反向读一张现有图片，推断它属于哪个朝代或画风，并给出改法。所有推荐颜色都来自数据集，技能从不凭空编造色值。也支持跨时代混搭，例如国风赛博朋克、水墨故障风。

- 补齐服饰形制、色阶规矩与常见错误清单
- 按主体/风格/媒介/光线分段组装英文提示词，复制即用
- 模式 C：读现有图片反推朝代画风，并给出具体修改建议

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a story premise (era, characters, scene, mood) or an existing image into an executable AI art-direction package: a style decision naming the dynasty or style lineage with its visual evidence; a palette of traditional Chinese colors with real CMYK/RGB values and proportions; the costume forms, color-rank constraints and mistakes to avoid; and ready-to-paste English prompts assembled by subject, style, medium and light. The data assets are self-contained in the skill's own data/ folder, with 384 traditional colors organized by the 24 solar terms and tied to their source chapters and book pages. Mode C reverse-engineers an existing image, infers its era or style and proposes concrete fixes. Every recommended color comes from that dataset — the skill never invents color values — and cross-era fusion such as Chinese-aesthetic cyberpunk or ink-wash glitch is supported.

### ✨ What it does

- Premise to dynasty or style decision with visual evidence, straight through to image prompts
- 384 traditional colors with CMYK/RGB values and documented sources, organized by the 24 solar terms
- Supplies palette proportions instead of just dumping a list of hex codes
- Fills in costume forms, color-rank constraints and a list of mistakes to avoid
- Assembles English prompts by subject, style, medium and light, ready to paste
- Mode C reverse-engineers an existing image's era and style, then proposes concrete fixes
